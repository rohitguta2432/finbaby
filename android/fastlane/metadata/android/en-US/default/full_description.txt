FinBaby (Jama) is a calm, minimal personal finance tracker built for Indian middle-class users. Track expenses, set budgets with the 50/30/20 rule, auto-import bank SMS transactions, and get smart saving tips — all in one place.

Features:
• SMS auto-import — Reads bank SMS messages and categorizes transactions automatically
• Manual entry — Add income/expense with category, notes, and date
• 50/30/20 budgeting — Plan needs, wants, and savings
• Reports & charts — Donut by category, daily bars, monthly trends
• Smart tips — Personalized saving suggestions
• Full-text search across all transactions
• CSV export and JSON backup/restore
• Biometric lock (fingerprint / face)
• Daily reminders

No ads. No accounts. Your data stays on your device.
